Features

Everything you need to split fairly.

  • Know where you stand

    Home shows your overall balance in each currency, who you need to settle with, and where you stand in every group.

  • Groups for everything

    Create a group for a trip, a flat or an office, with its own default currency. Add expenses and members inside it.

  • Four ways to split

    Split equally, unequally with exact amounts, by percentage, or by shares. Amounts always add up to the paisa.

  • Smart settle-up

    See suggested payments between members, then tap Settle to record a payment when someone pays you back.

  • Add anyone

    Add friends, any OmniSplit user by email, or guests without an account. Guests link up when they join.

  • ₹ and 20+ currencies

    Indian rupees by default, with more than 20 other currencies. Balances are kept separately for each currency.

  • Export to CSV

    Download a group's expenses, settlements and balances as a CSV file, or share them as text.

  • Sign in your way

    Use your Google account, or an email and password. Email sign-ups are verified before they can log in.

  • Private by design

    No ads, and your data is never sold. Only you and the people in your groups can see your expenses.
