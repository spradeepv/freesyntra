OmniSplit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OmniSplit, an expense-splitting app for Android developed by FreeSyntra ("we", "us"), collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you use the app. By using OmniSplit, you agree to the practices described here.

In short

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account details (name and email) and the groups, friends and expenses you add.
  • We do not show ads, sell your data, or use it for advertising. The app has no advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, or bank, UPI or card details.
  • OmniSplit records who paid and who owes what. It does not move money or process payments.
  • Your data is stored with Google Firebase and is visible only to you and the people you share groups with.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. See Delete your OmniSplit account.

Information we collect

Information you give us

TypeDetails
AccountYour name and email address. If you sign up with email and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account.
GroupsGroup names, default currency, the group's members, and who created the group.
Expenses and settlementsDescription, amount, currency, date, who paid, how it was split (equally, unequally, by percentage or by shares) and each person's share, plus payments you record to settle up.
FriendsThe names and, optionally, email addresses of friends you add, including guests who don't have an OmniSplit account.

Information collected automatically

When the app connects to Firebase, Google's servers receive technical information needed to provide the service and keep it secure, such as your IP address, device and operating-system details, and app instance identifiers. OmniSplit does not include advertising or analytics tools, and we do not use this information to track you.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, precise or approximate location, photos or files, call logs, SMS, or bank, UPI or card details.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, and to send the email-verification and password-reset emails you ask for.
  • To store your groups, friends and expenses and sync them across your devices.
  • To calculate balances and suggest settlements between group members.
  • To let you find and add friends by email, and to link a guest you added to their OmniSplit account once they sign up with the email you entered.
  • To create CSV exports when you ask for them. Exported files are saved where you choose on your device or shared through apps you pick; we do not receive a copy.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it.

Who can see your information

  • Group members can see the group's name, its members' names and emails, and all of its expenses, balances and settlements.
  • Friends: when someone adds you as a friend, your name and email appear in their friends list, and theirs in yours.
  • Finding accounts by email: another signed-in OmniSplit user who enters your exact email address can find your account and see your name, so they can add you as a friend or group member.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.

We do not share your information with anyone else.

Service providers

OmniSplit uses these Google services, which process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication handles sign-in, email verification and password reset.
  • Cloud Firestore (Firebase) is the database that stores your profile, friends, groups and expenses.
  • Google Sign-In (Credential Manager) lets you sign in with your Google account.

Their handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase. Data may be processed on Google servers in India or other countries where Google operates.

Information about other people

When you add friends, guests or group members, you provide their names and, optionally, their email addresses. Please add only people you actually share expenses with, and only use email addresses they would be comfortable sharing. If someone else added you to OmniSplit and you want your details removed, ask them to remove you, or contact us.

Storage and security

  • Data travels between the app and Firebase over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and is encrypted at rest by Google.
  • Database security rules limit access: you can read and change only your own profile and friends list, and only the groups you are a member of. Only a group's creator can delete the group.
  • The app keeps a local copy of your data on your phone so it works offline. Changes made offline sync when you reconnect.
  • Your phone's own backup. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include OmniSplit's data on this phone in that backup, which Google stores in your Google account. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings, not by us.
  • No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Data retention and deletion

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • You can delete expenses and friends at any time, and you can delete groups you created. Deleting a group deletes all of its expenses for every member.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, go to About → Delete account. Your account, profile and friends list are deleted immediately, along with the groups you created and their expenses, and you are removed from other groups. You can also ask us by email. Full steps are on Delete your OmniSplit account.
  • What we keep: expenses in other people's groups that involve you stay in those groups under your name, so their balances remain correct, unless you ask us to replace your name with "Former member". We keep other information only if the law requires it, and only for as long as it requires.
  • Google, which hosts our servers, removes any remaining copies from its backup systems on its own schedule, generally within 180 days.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it (you can change most of it directly in the app);
  • have it erased;
  • withdraw your consent, which you can do by deleting your account;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or are unable to do so; and
  • raise a grievance with us and, if it isn't resolved, complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, contact us. We respond within 30 days.

Children

OmniSplit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Users under 18 should use the app only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we change the "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app or by email. If you keep using OmniSplit after a change, the updated policy applies.
